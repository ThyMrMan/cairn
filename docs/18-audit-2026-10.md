# 18 — Audit, October 2026

A read-only review of the whole tool, done before building the Wayback pre-fill ([17](17-wayback-prefill.md)). It covered five areas: capture core, discovery/feeds/replay/search, API/DB/security/deployment, frontend, and **real crawl evidence** from `Cairn_Docker/logs-checking-status/cairn-diagnostic.db` (104 jobs, 80 captures, 908,802 URL rows, snapshot ~2026-09-16) plus the three saved crawl logs.

**Status labels:**
- **Confirmed:** traced in code and, where marked, re-checked by hand.
- **Likely:** needs a probe to confirm.

**Effort:** S is under half a day, M is one to three days, L is longer.

The headline: the code is careful and well-tested in the small, but in production **most crawl time was wasted, not spent archiving**. The four biggest drains are loops, URL sprawl, sleeping on CDN assets, and crawls that die without keeping their work. They all have fixes of modest size.

## Live data check, 2026-10-30

This re-ran the analysis on a read-only copy of the live `/config/cairn.db`, covering everything since the old snapshot (2026-09-17 to 2026-10-30). In that window: 96 sites, 312 captures, 1.76 M URL rows and **915 capture-hours**. The copy was deleted afterwards.

## P0 — Crawls that waste days or lose their work

| # | Finding | Evidence | Fix | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Re-fetch loops are detected but never stopped** | Capture 61: **381,126 fetches of 4,011 distinct URLs (95×)** over 5.4 days (re-checked). Capture 64: 209k fetches of 2,785 URLs (75×). `services/crawlhealth.py:42` `LOOP_RATIO=3.0` is only read by the polling endpoint (`api/routers/jobs.py:219`) and after the crawl (`postprocess.py:1214`). | The supervisor watches `repetition()` and auto-pauses once it stays ≥3× over 2+ windows. Also fix the cause from commit 3b7bc21: wget re-requests any URL that wrote no file. | M |
| 2 | **Preset label and actual reject patterns drift apart** | 10 of 22 sites labelled `blogger-lean` lack the pager reject, and those are the sprawling ones. Job 92: **521,575 of 539,846 log lines were `/search?updated-max…` pages** (re-checked). 20 of 24 sites still carry the retired `[?&]m=1` instead of `m=[01]`. Retirements run only in `apply_preset_to_scope` (`discovery_service.py:200`). | At boot or capture start, reconcile each scope with its preset: apply retirements, and warn about missing preset rejects. | S–M |
| 3 | **Captures are seeded with only the first 200 discovered URLs** | `discovery_service.py:50` stores `all_urls[:200]` as `sample_urls`, and `seeds_for_capture` (`:254`) seeds from it. `MAX_SEEDS=50_000` can never be reached, and the comment promising to "re-derive the rest" isn't implemented (re-checked). | Store the full list in a sidecar file (`index/discovered-urls.txt.gz`) and read it when building seeds. Label feed URLs as `feed`, not `sitemap`. | S–M |
| 4 | **Interrupted crawls lose their work; the duration cap is engine-side only** | 5 jobs ended `interrupted` after **588 h of crawling** (jobs 92: 245 h, 103: 189 h, 74: 91 h, 85: 46 h, 41: 13 h; re-checked). Capture 84: ~157 GB fetched, none registered. Only `paused` captures resume (`jobs.py:2967`), and `_recover_interrupted` (`:267`) just relabels them. The 48 h cap lives in `wget.py:397`, so a wedged engine ignores it. | On boot, register and index the WARCs an interrupted capture already wrote (as `partial`) and offer resume. Add a duration watchdog in the supervisor. | M |
| 5 | **No rate-limit handling anywhere outbound** | wget: fixed `--tries=3 --waitretry=10`, no `--retry-on-http-error`, no breaker (`wget.py:208-216`, re-checked). 429 bodies are stored (`--content-on-error`) and indexed, and can shadow good captures in replay. Discovery: `DiscoveryOptions` gets no `wait_s` (`jobs.py:787`), and a 429 on sampling returns `[]` silently (`runner.py:423`). Real data shows only 32×503 and 8×429, so this matters mainly for the hard-limited site being targeted next. | wget gets `--retry-on-http-error=429,503`, a configurable `--waitretry`, and a consecutive-429 breaker that stops with `partial/rate_limited`. Discovery uses the site's `wait_s` and a 429-aware `Fetcher`. Keep 429/503 records out of replay. One 429-aware fetcher can serve discovery, feeds and the Wayback import. | M |
| 6 | **A sitemap fetch failure marks live URLs "gone"** | `discovery/sources.py:225`: a failed `?page=N` just `break`s with no error (re-checked). `feeds.py:368` then treats the walk as complete, `_mark_gone` flags the unread URLs, and a `URLS_DISAPPEARED` notification is sent. | Add a `complete` flag to `UrlSource`, set false on pagination failure or a cap. | S |
| 7 | **A restart deletes an in-flight browsertrix crawl** | Its WARCs live in `tmp/job-N/crawls` until `_collect` (`browsertrix.py:149, 487`). `sweep_tmp` runs at boot (`app.py:93`), and `_remove_tree(temp_dir)` runs on stop (`jobs.py:706`). Compose and Unraid set no stop grace period, so Docker kills after 10 s. | Keep the crawl directory under `captures/<dir>/.inflight/` and collect it on boot. Add `stop_grace_period: 120s` to compose and `--stop-timeout` to the Unraid template. | M |

## P1 — Security and data safety

| # | Finding | Evidence | Fix | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 8 | **Replay server has no authentication** | pywb binds `0.0.0.0:8081` (`docker/rootfs/.../pywb/run:26`) and compose publishes 8081 (re-checked). `enable_cdx_api` lets anyone who can reach it list every URL, including pages captured with logged-in cookie profiles. There's no Host allowlist, so DNS rebinding is possible. | Bind pywb to 127.0.0.1 and put an authenticated gate in front (an app proxy with a signed token, or proxy `auth_request`). At minimum add a Host allowlist, disable the CDX API, and document the exposure. | M–L |
| 9 | **DB backups rotate away the pre-upgrade copy in a restart loop** | `run_migrations` backs up on every boot, unconditionally (`db/bootstrap.py:160-164`, re-checked), and is reached from both `cairn migrate` and app startup. Only 10 copies are kept. | Back up only when `current != head`, with the revision in the filename. Never rotate out the newest pre-migration copy. | S |
| 10 | **Docs promise a recovery that doesn't exist** | docs/10:331 points to `POST /api/maintenance/rebuild-db`, which docs/09:463 says was dropped. docs/09:460 says `cp` the DB, but docs/10:329 says a live copy of a WAL database is corrupt. | Correct both. Add `cairn backup` (`VACUUM INTO`), runnable on a schedule. | S |
| 11 | **One unreadable WARC blocks a site's replay index permanently** | `cdxj_lines` raises on any WARC (`replay.py:642-648`), so the state file is never written and every later update fails the same way. Postprocess turns it into a warning only. | Index per WARC with try/except, record failed files in `site.cdxj.json`, continue. Make the integrity check two-way (WARCs on disk but missing from the index). | S |
| 12 | **Disk-full and I/O errors are reported as success** | Any exit code with `urls>0` becomes `partial` (`wget.py:444-449`, re-checked), and the job then closes `ok`. `free_space_floor_bytes` is hard-coded `None` (`jobs.py:3044`). | Treat exits 1 and 3 as failures with a reason. Add a free-space check to the poll loop. | S |
| 13 | **A resumed capture forgets the first half's WARCs** | `_finalize` sets `warc_files` from this run's events only (`jobs.py:2041`), so checksums, manifest and integrity skip the pre-pause files. The page cap also restarts from zero. | Merge with the existing list on resume, or register every WARC in `out/warc`. | S |
| 14 | **wget's WARC on SIGTERM** *(likely)* | `wget.py:313` assumes wget closes the current record on TERM. GNU wget doesn't handle SIGTERM. Cancel, pause and caps may leave a truncated final gzip member and log-only URLs miscounted as revisits. | Probe with `scripts/probes`. If confirmed, truncate to the last complete member using CDX offsets. | S–M |
| 15 | **Shutdown during post-processing mislabels the capture** | The CancelledError path `_fail`s a capture whose post-processing thread is still running (`jobs.py:2170`). Interrupted post-processing never re-runs. | Don't fail a capture once its record step has run. Queue "finish post-processing" at boot. | S–M |
| 16 | Smaller security items | `POST /sites/{id}/reindex` lacks CSRF (`api/routers/replay.py:107`; re-checked: the router has no `Csrf`). `client_ip` trusts the leftmost XFF (`api/deps.py:84`). Anyone can keep the single account locked out (`auth.py:202`). The CSP cache is keyed on the raw Host header with no bound (`middleware.py:93`). TOTP codes can be replayed. SSE outlives logout. First-run setup is open (DNS rebinding). | Each is a small fix. `TrustedHostMiddleware` covers two of them. | S each |
| 17 | Async routes write to the DB on the event loop | `cancel_job`, `pause_job` and interactive/profile routes call `db.commit()` in `async def`. A 5 s busy timeout during post-processing stalls every request, SSE stream and healthcheck, then returns a generic 500. | Make them `def` or use `run_in_threadpool`. Map busy errors to 503 with `Retry-After`. | S–M |

## P4 — Hygiene and tests

- **Builds:** no Python lockfile, only `>=` ranges, with hand-tuned pywb/werkzeug overrides. Compose uses `:latest`. The healthcheck ignores pywb. `npm run lint` fails because ESLint isn't installed.
- **Local test environment is broken:** `argon2` is missing, so the suite fails at import. The local wget is 1.21.4 (mingw), but the code's measurements are pinned to 1.25.0. pywb isn't on PATH, so the replay e2e tests skip.
- **Coverage gaps:** loop auto-stop (#1), preset drift (#2), salvaging interrupted captures (#4), the supervisor-side duration cap, and per-host politeness.
- **Size:** `services/jobs.py` is 3,219 lines and `_prepare` is a 220-line branch-on-kind. Frontend `api.ts`, `Settings.tsx` and `SiteDetail.tsx` are each over 1,200 lines.

## Prerequisites for the Wayback pre-fill

These block or distort [17](17-wayback-prefill.md) and should land first:

- **#3 (200-URL seed cap):** the gap crawl's seed list would be truncated the same way.
- **#13 (resume forgets WARCs):** the import is designed around pause/resume.
- **Per-host busy set:** it keys on `site.primary_host` (`jobs.py:2888-2910`). Let a job declare the hosts it hits, so an import serialises on `web.archive.org` and not on the origin.
- **`_dedup_cdx` returns `None` for `kind=="full"`:** a gap crawl must still dedup against imported records.
- **#5's 429-aware fetcher:** build it once and reuse it for discovery, feeds and the CDX/`id_` client.

## Suggested order

1. **Quick wins, about a day in total:** #2, #3, #6, #9, #11, #12, #19, #22, #25, and the reindex CSRF fix. They are cheap and each stops real waste or a silent failure.
2. **Crawl control:** #1 (loop auto-stop), #4 (salvage and watchdog), #5 (rate-limit handling), #18 (asset pass). This is the bulk of the time savings.
3. **Protect the archive:** #8 (replay auth), #7, #13, #14, #15.
4. Wayback prerequisites, then [17](17-wayback-prefill.md).
5. Frontend P3 and hygiene alongside.
